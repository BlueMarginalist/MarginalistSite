EARL translates official U.S. economic-account concepts (from the Bureau of
Economic Analysis) into a business-facing income-statement framework, so the
economy can be read the way an analyst reads a company.

## The mapping

- **Gross output** → Revenue
- **Intermediate inputs** → COGS
- **Gross value added** → Gross profit

## What it offers

- Validated National, Corporate, and Industry income statements, quarterly and annual
- Charts of the principal economic-account measures
- Downloadable datasets, release files, and metadata
- A recurring statistical release (National + Corporate Income), with archive and PDF

## Method

Business-facing labels are analytical translations of official BEA concepts;
the site documents definitions, construction, and source lineage.
